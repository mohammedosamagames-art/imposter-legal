Delete your account & data

App: The Imposter (مين الكذاب) · Package: com.hamo.whoistheliar
Developer: ACGROUP · Contact: mohammedosamagames@gmail.com

Fastest way — inside the app:
Open The Imposter → tap Settings (⚙️) on the home screen → tap Delete Account (حذف الحساب نهائيًا) → confirm.

What deleting does

When you confirm deletion in the app, it immediately:

  • Leaves any active room and closes all voice connections.
  • Deletes your Firebase anonymous account.
  • Removes your profile and progress data (points, level, XP, owned characters, badges, daily challenge, streak, display name and avatar) and your block list.
  • Deletes the text chat messages you sent from this device that still exist in rooms, and your global leaderboard entry.
  • Wipes everything the app stored on your device, and resets the app to a fresh state.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is kept, and how to remove it

Moderation reports (filed by you or about you) and gift-code redemption records are kept for safety and abuse prevention. They contain only an anonymous identifier, and after deletion that identifier no longer points to any account. Chat messages you sent from another device or an earlier install are not removed by the app either. You can ask us to delete any of these (below).

Requesting deletion of remaining records (or if you already uninstalled the app)

Email us at mohammedosamagames@gmail.com from the address you want associated with the request. Include any account identifier you have (the app shows your Account ID on the deletion confirmation screen). We will locate and delete the data associated with your request (profile, reports, gift-code records, leaderboard entry, chat messages). We aim to complete valid deletion requests within 30 days.

Local (offline) play

Local pass-and-play on a single device does not create an online profile. Data from local play lives only on that device and is removed when you delete your account in the app or clear the app's storage.
