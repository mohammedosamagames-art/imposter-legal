Delete your account & data

App: The Imposter (مين الكذاب) · Package: com.hamo.whoistheliar
Developer: ACGROUP · Contact: mohammedosamagames@gmail.com

Fastest way — inside the app:
Open The Imposter → tap Settings (⚙️) on the home screen → tap Delete Account (حذف الحساب نهائيًا) → confirm.

What deleting does

When you confirm deletion in the app, it immediately:

  • Leaves any active room and closes all voice connections.
  • Deletes your Firebase anonymous account.
  • Removes your profile and progress data (points, level, XP, owned characters, badges, daily challenge, streak, display name and avatar).
  • Wipes everything the app stored on your device, and resets the app to a fresh state.

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Server-side records (honest note about timing)

A small number of records are managed by our backend rather than your device — for example a global leaderboard entry and any moderation reports you submitted. Deletion of these is started immediately when you delete your account. Where a record cannot be erased instantly, it is detached from your account and removed during our normal processing cycle. We do not claim that every server record disappears the same second you press the button, but your account and identity are removed and the remaining records are deleted as part of the same process.

If you already uninstalled the app

If you can no longer open the in-app deletion button, email us at mohammedosamagames@gmail.com from the address you want associated with the request. Include any account identifier you have (the app shows your Account ID on the deletion confirmation screen). We will locate and delete the server-side data associated with your request. We aim to complete valid deletion requests within 30 days.

Local (offline) play

Local pass-and-play on a single device does not create an online profile. Data from local play lives only on that device and is removed when you delete your account in the app or clear the app's storage.
